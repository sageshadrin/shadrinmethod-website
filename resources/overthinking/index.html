Overthinking: What Happens Inside

Articles›Overthinking

Sometimes thoughts return to the same situation, even after we have thought it through. Behind this familiar mental loop may be different inner states: anxiety, shame, resentment, fear of making a mistake, or painful uncertainty.

This is where the work begins—with understanding your own inner state. Here you can identify the experience that feels familiar, understand how it relates to recurring thoughts, and explore a practical approach to working through that inner reaction and breaking out of the cycle.

What stays with you?

Choose the situation that feels familiar. Recognizing what you're experiencing is a first step toward working with it.
