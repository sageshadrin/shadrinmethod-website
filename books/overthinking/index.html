Do your thoughts keep circling back?

You may replay a conversation long after it ends, revisit choices you have already made, or mentally prepare for a future that has not arrived. Thinking can be useful. But a loop can leave you exhausted without bringing you any closer to a decision.

Overthinking: Free Yourself From Mental Replay explores this difference and offers a practical approach to the recurring patterns behind mental replay.

Three familiar forms of overthinking

Replaying the past

When a finished conversation or event keeps returning in your thoughts.

Second-guessing decisions

When a choice has been made, yet doubt repeatedly reopens it.

Rehearsing the future

When preparing for tomorrow turns into endless imagined scenarios.

A practical book, not another demand to “just stop thinking”

The goal is not to switch off your mind or never feel uncertain again. The book introduces the Shadrin Pattern Method as an approach to recognizing recurring mental loops and exploring the reactions that may keep them active.

It is designed as a standalone guide. You do not need to read another book in the series first, and the practical exercises remain inside the book rather than being reproduced here.
