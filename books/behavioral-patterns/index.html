How Behavioral Patterns Hinder Us on the Path to Achieving Success

When familiar reactions get in the way

Why can we understand our goals and still repeat behaviors that interfere with reaching them? This book explores recurring patterns, inner resistance, and ways to approach familiar situations differently.

A standalone book for readers interested in practical self-exploration of recurring behavioral reactions.

Inside the book

Behavioral patterns can appear as delayed decisions, recurring reactions, and difficulty changing even when we genuinely want to move forward. The book presents the author's perspective on these patterns and a practical way to explore them.
